End of game: win / lose modal

The modal opens over the board on every phone and on the host screen. Winners read "You win!", the others read the winning team's name. Three animation ideas. Press Replay.

A · Confetti

Winners: confetti rains in the app colours and the card pops in with a yellow glow. Others: the card pops in calmly, no confetti.

B · Gold record

Winners: a gold record spins in and keeps turning. Others: the record winds down and stops, slightly greyed.

C · Spotlight

Winners: a yellow spotlight sweeps onto the card with a light sprinkle of confetti. Others: the screen dims.
